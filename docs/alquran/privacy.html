Privacy Policy

Al Quran Mobile Application

Last Updated:

📱 Introduction

Welcome to Al Quran App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, secure experience while using our Islamic application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle your information when you use our Al Quran mobile application.

Our Commitment: We respect your privacy and are dedicated to protecting your personal information while providing you with the best Quranic reading experience.
🔍 Information We Collect ▼

Personal Information

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, reading preferences, bookmarks, and reading history
  • Settings & Preferences: Language preferences, font size, audio settings, notification preferences
  • Technical Data: IP address, crash reports, performance data

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number) unless voluntarily provided
  • Location data beyond country/region for localization
  • Financial information
  • Sensitive personal data
📊 How We Use Your Information ▼

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality: To provide core features like Quran reading, search, bookmarks, and audio playback
  • Personalization: To remember your reading preferences, font settings, and last read position
  • Performance Improvement: To analyze app usage and improve performance and user experience
  • Prayer Times: To provide accurate prayer times based on your location (if permitted)
  • Offline Functionality: To enable reading and listening without internet connection
  • Bug Fixes: To identify and fix technical issues
🔐 Data Protection & Security ▼

We implement various security measures to protect your information:

  • Local Storage: Most data is stored locally on your device
  • Encryption: Data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard protocols
  • Limited Access: Only authorized personnel have access to user data
  • Regular Updates: We regularly update our security measures
  • No Selling: We never sell your personal information to third parties
🤝 Information Sharing ▼

We Do NOT Share Your Information Except:

  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or legal process
  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party services that help us operate the app (analytics, crash reporting)
  • Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users

Third-Party Services We May Use:

  • Google Analytics (for usage statistics)
  • Firebase (for app functionality and crash reporting)
  • Ad networks (if applicable, clearly disclosed)
👤 Your Rights & Choices ▼

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request information about data we have collected
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out: Opt-out of data collection for analytics
  • App Permissions: Control app permissions through device settings

How to Exercise Your Rights:

  • Contact us directly through the app or email
  • Use in-app privacy settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data
👶 Children's Privacy ▼

Our app is designed to be family-friendly and suitable for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

The app contains Islamic educational content that is appropriate for children and encourages learning about the Quran in a safe environment.

🔄 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Continued use of the app after any modifications to this Privacy Policy constitutes acceptance of those changes.

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please don't hesitate to contact us:

Language Support: English, Arabic, and other supported languages

🕌 Islamic Values & Ethics

Our privacy practices are guided by Islamic values of honesty, transparency, and respect for individuals. We believe in treating user data with the same care and respect we would want for our own information.
