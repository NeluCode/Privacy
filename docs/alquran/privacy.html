1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Al Quran App ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing you with access to the Holy Quran and related Islamic content.

We prioritize transparency and user control over personal data while maintaining the highest standards of data protection.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Information

Device Information

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Device language settings
  • Screen resolution

Usage Analytics

  • App launch frequency
  • Feature usage patterns
  • Session duration
  • Navigation paths
  • Performance metrics

2.2 User-Provided Information

  • Preferences: Font size, audio settings, reading preferences
  • Bookmarks: Saved verses, chapters, or pages
  • Reading History: Last read positions and progress tracking
  • Prayer Times: Location data for accurate prayer time calculations (optional)

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identification information without explicit consent
  • Financial or payment information
  • Contacts or address book data
  • Camera or microphone access beyond app functionality
  • Social media profiles or activity

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Primary Purposes

  • Core Functionality: Provide Quranic text, translations, and audio recitations
  • Personalization: Remember user preferences and settings
  • Prayer Times: Calculate accurate prayer times based on location
  • Offline Access: Enable content access without internet connectivity
  • Search: Provide search functionality across Quranic content

3.2 Secondary Purposes

  • Analytics: Understand user behavior to improve app functionality
  • Performance: Monitor and enhance app performance and stability
  • Support: Provide technical support and troubleshooting
  • Legal Compliance: Meet legal obligations and protect user rights

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist in app operations:

  • Analytics Services: Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics (aggregated data only)
  • Cloud Storage: Secure cloud services for app functionality
  • Crash Reporting: Technical error reporting to improve stability

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law or to:

  • Comply with legal processes or government requests
  • Protect our rights, property, or safety
  • Protect user safety and security
  • Investigate potential violations of our terms

Important: We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Security and Protection

5.1 Security Measures

  • Encryption: Data transmission secured with industry-standard encryption
  • Local Storage: Most data stored securely on your device
  • Access Controls: Restricted access to user data
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security assessments and updates
  • Data Minimization: Collection limited to necessary information only

5.2 Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations. User preferences and bookmarks are stored locally and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 Access and Control

  • Access: Request information about data we have collected
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data
  • Objection: Object to certain data processing activities

6.2 App Permissions

You can control app permissions through your device settings:

  • Location: Required only for prayer time calculations
  • Storage: Used for offline content and user preferences
  • Network: Required for content updates and analytics

6.3 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise your privacy rights, contact us using the information provided in the Contact section.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is designed to be suitable for users of all ages, including children, as it contains educational Islamic content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to remove such information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify users of material changes through the app or other appropriate means.

Your continued use of the app after any modifications constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
