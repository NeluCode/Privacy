Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 8, 2025

Welcome to Bite AI! We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage when you use the Bite AI mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect information such as your name, email address, and profile details.
  • Food Images: Photos of food you capture or select from your device gallery for calorie tracking and nutritional analysis.
  • Barcode Data: Product barcodes you scan using our in-app scanner for food identification.
  • Fitness Data: Workout information and step count data from your Android device's fitness sensors.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app, including features used and time spent.
  • Location Data: We may collect approximate location data to provide location-based features (with your permission).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Calorie Tracking: Processing food images through AI to identify food items and estimate nutritional values.
  • Barcode Scanning: Using Google ML Kit to scan product barcodes and retrieve nutritional information.
  • Fitness Tracking: Analyzing your workout and step data to provide insights on your activity levels and calorie expenditure.
  • Personalization: Customizing your experience based on your dietary preferences and fitness goals.
  • Notifications: Sending you reminders, updates, and personalized recommendations.
  • Service Improvement: Analyzing usage patterns to improve app functionality and user experience.
  • Data Synchronization: Syncing your data across devices using Firebase services.

3. Permissions Required

Bite AI requires the following permissions to function properly:

  • Camera: To capture photos of food for calorie analysis and scan barcodes.
  • Storage/Photos: To allow you to select existing photos from your gallery.
  • Internet: To send images to our servers for processing and sync data with Firebase.
  • Notifications: To send you reminders, meal logging prompts, and app updates.
  • Physical Activity: To access workout and step count data from Android fitness sensors.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take your data security seriously:

  • Firebase Integration: Your data is securely stored and synchronized using Google Firebase services.
  • Encryption: Data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard protocols.
  • Server Security: Food images sent to our servers for AI processing are handled securely and deleted after processing is complete.
  • Access Controls: We implement strict access controls to ensure only authorized personnel can access user data.

5. Third-Party Services

Bite AI integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google ML Kit: For barcode scanning functionality. Google's privacy policy applies to this service.
  • Firebase (Google): For data storage, authentication, and synchronization. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • AI Processing Servers: Food images are sent to our secure servers for nutritional analysis using AI technology.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share specific information.
  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party service providers who assist in operating our app (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics).
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental regulation.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred to the acquiring entity.

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Data Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Update or correct your information within the app
  • Request deletion of your data (see section 7.2)

7.2 Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time. To delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Open the Bite AI app
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Select "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your deletion request

Alternatively, you can email us at nelucode+biteai@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and we will process your request within 30 days.
